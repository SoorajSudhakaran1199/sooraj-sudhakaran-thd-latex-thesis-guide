\section{Design definition}
\label{sec:design}
The fictional product consists of two containers and a connecting opening.
A real design chapter would specify dimensions, materials, interfaces and assembly
tolerances. Here those details are deliberately absent because no device was built.

\section{Risk and reproducibility}
An illustrative failure mode is leakage at the joint. A real development process
would assess its consequences, likelihood, detection and mitigation before testing.
Versioned drawings and an assembly procedure would support reproducibility.
The model in Chapter~\ref{chap:simulation} illustrates the analysis step.
