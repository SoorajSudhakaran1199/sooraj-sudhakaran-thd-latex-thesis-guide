\section{Measurable acceptance criterion}
\label{sec:requirements}
Requirement R1 states that the mean of the three illustrative durations shall
be within \qty{2}{\second} of a target of \qty{60}{\second}.
The criterion is evaluated in Chapter~\ref{chap:verification_of_prototype}.
For an actual study, a deadline, operating conditions and a justified sample size
would be agreed before data collection.
