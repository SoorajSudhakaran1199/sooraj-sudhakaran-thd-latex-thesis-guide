\section{Background and objective}
\label{sec:background}
This fictional study concerns a classroom sand timer. Its purpose is to teach
how an engineering argument connects a requirement, a design and a verification.
No real product is investigated.

\subsection{Illustrative hypothesis}
\label{sec:hypothesis}
The illustrative hypothesis is that concept A gives a smaller mean duration
error than concept B under identical conditions. This is a teaching proposition,
not a claim of research novelty. A real thesis must identify an unresolved,
measurable research hypothesis beyond the existing state of the art.

\section{Structure of the thesis}
Chapter~\ref{chap:state_of_the_art} establishes the background.
Requirements guide concept selection and development. Simulation precedes
prototype realization. Chapter~\ref{chap:verification_of_prototype} presents the
constructed results; the conclusion addresses their limitations and next steps.
